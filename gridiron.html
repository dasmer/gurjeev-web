      Game rules & controls

      Offense-only arcade football. In a season, play every division rival twice. Finish with a winning record, then win the divisional playoff and conference championship to reach the Super Bowl. Four 90-second quarters; your defense plays automatically while you follow the drive. Touchdowns: 6 points, automatic extra point: 1. Field goals: 3. Safety: 2. Ties go to sudden-death overtime. Drag from the quarterback toward an open receiver. Defenders can jump the pass and run it back. Your players pursue the return automatically. During runs use ↑/↓ or W/S, or drag vertically. Space dives. R tucks and runs; H hands off. Escape pauses. Choose routes before each snap. Pick from the 32 NFL teams or Gurjeev's dream team. The visiting team wears white if the jerseys clash. The Super Bowl is played at a neutral stadium. Other playoff games use fictional team ratings and simulated scores. Gurjeev's dream team is a fantasy roster. The other teams use fictional players and team ratings are equal.
